The perfect summer song. Everyone knows it. Everyone loves it. This song bring out so much joy in me I literally cannot explain how much I love it. If I could inject this song into my veins I would. When this song first came out I remember instantly loving it. 

This song reminds me of a friendship more than a love song. It reminds me of summer nights spent with the people who make me the happiest in this life. The lyrics "funny how a melody sounds like a memory" I cannot describe how these lyrics just get it.
